Ground-breaking & High-lighting
2025
University Hall (大学会館), Tokyo University of the Arts
MFA Graduation Exhibition — GAP Award 2025
Shared space with Juli Lee
Hal and Juli got the chance to use University Hall (大学会館) of Tokyo University of the Arts for their MFA graduation show. They decided to share and co-work in the space, renovating one wall of the room that hadn't been renovated for decades, using the materials from the wall and its structure to compose something. They brought their own perspectives — ground and infrastructure, weather and light.
